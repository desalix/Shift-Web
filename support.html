My entries aren't syncing between devices

  • Check both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • Make sure iCloud is on for Shift: Settings → [your name] → iCloud, and Shift is allowed to use it.
  • You need an internet connection. Changes can take a minute or two to appear.

How do I add a shift quickly?

Tap + and type into Quick add, for example “work thursday 9-17 at 12.50/h”. The form fills itself — check it, then save.

How do I delete an entry, or a whole series?

Open the day and swipe left on the entry. If it repeats, Shift asks whether to delete just that one or the whole series.

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings → Export, choose one or more months, and share the file wherever you like.

Widget and shortcut

Add the Shift widget from your Home Screen to jump straight to a new entry. You can also long-press the app icon and choose New Entry.

Languages

Shift is available in English and Spanish. It follows your device language, or you can pick one in Settings → Language.

Privacy
